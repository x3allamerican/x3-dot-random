DOT random selection

Paste your random pool, one driver ID per line (use employee numbers, not names or SSNs). See how many drug and alcohol tests this period needs to stay on pace for FMCSA's 50% and 10% minimums, then make an unbiased draw you can prove to an auditor. Runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Pool and period

Selection

  • Press "Draw Drivers".

Decision support, not legal advice. Each driver has an equal chance every draw; drivers tested earlier stay in the pool (49 CFR 382.305(i)). Rates: FMCSA 2026, 50% drug and 10% alcohol. If you use a consortium (C/TPA), it must test at these rates for the whole pool.

Free and open source (MIT) from X3 Fleet Safety: driver qualification, hours of service, drug and alcohol, CSA and the rest of your DOT program in one place.
